# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Small businesses and independent professionals install these themes in Cress
sites. Visitors read their work, learn about their services, and find contact
information.

Iris serves photographers, artists, and tattoo studios. Their visitors browse
visual work and open individual projects before making contact.

## Product purpose

Provide standalone Cress themes that business owners can install without
frontend development.

Iris adds an image-first portfolio to the collection. Artwork leads the page;
the interface supports browsing and contact.

## Operating context

Themes use Cress templates, local CSS, and English and German labels.
Owners manage content through Cress rather than editing the theme.

## Capabilities and constraints

Themes support Cress 1.x from 1.0.0 and provide landing, list, ordinary, and
not-found pages.

Iris uses existing Cress features for its landing page, portfolio index, and
project pages. It preserves image proportions and has no mandatory animation,
carousel, or autoplay. A dedicated gallery is outside the first version.

## Brand commitments

Theme names follow the collection's plant naming. The new theme is Iris.
Each theme has its own visual identity rather than applying the Cozy Badger
company palette to every customer site.

## Evidence on hand

Existing theme packages under `themes/` establish Cress integration patterns.
The collection README documents installation and validation commands.

The Iris demo will use appropriately licensed sample images with attribution.
No customer artwork or customer claims have been supplied.

## Product principles

- Keep installation and content authoring simple.
- Let artwork lead without competing decorative effects.
- Preserve usable reading, navigation, and contact on small screens.
- Test browser layout without relying on fragile content fragmentation.
