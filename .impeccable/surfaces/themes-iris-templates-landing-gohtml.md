Iris landing page and portfolio. Mode: Experience.

Visitors browse visual work, open projects, and find contact information.
Use existing Cress page types, preserve image proportions and DOM order, and
keep every link and caption visible without hover. No mandatory animation.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A pasted street-poster wall. Oversized condensed lettering and
acid-yellow fields announce the work; dense, asymmetric images lead the page.
The user rejected the quiet monograph and explicitly approved this replacement.

OWN-WORLD: Acid-yellow stock, near-black ink, white reading sheets, locally
bundled Anton display lettering, and blunt sans captions. Hard section cuts,
large type, and offset image placement replace delicate editorial margins.
No fake graffiti, decorative distressed masks, or artwork overlays.

STORY: Browse the work, read its credit, open a project, then find contact.
The preview is explicitly a sample portfolio using credited urban photographs
and abstract artwork, never claims about the demo studio's own projects.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A bold horizontal nameplate and underlined navigation on acid
stock. A full-width uppercase title precedes an oversized urban photograph;
an ink-black excerpt panel occupies the image's right-hand margin without
covering it. Small credits remain beside the work. Portfolio/contact links
are obvious. On phones the same sequence stacks without image cropping.

FORM: Street-poster wall, explicitly pinned by the user, replacing the former
artist monograph. seed:5670e5645b7265eb. Code-first remains confirmed. The new direction round's
catalog alternatives do not override the pinned brief.
Signature interaction: portfolio titles reverse into acid-yellow ink panels
on hover/focus, without moving or recoloring the artwork. Reduced motion
makes that change immediate.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the
finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its
provenance.
